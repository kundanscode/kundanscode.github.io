01 — About

A brief introduction.

Four years across fintech and event-tech, one obsession: making complex distributed systems feel effortless.

I started with a diploma in engineering and built my way up through curiosity — first into a B.Tech, then into production systems that serve thousands of users daily. Today I work across 30+ microservices at Kayana, owning features from API contract design through production rollout.

My work sits at the intersection of backend rigor and AI experimentation. I've built Kafka-driven event pipelines that improved system resilience under load, designed a recurring-ticket system with QR verification and privacy controls, and recently shipped agentic AI workflows that chain OCR, LLM extraction, and model-agnostic routing into production automation.

I'm a daily user of AI coding assistants — Claude, IDE-based LLM tooling — not as a shortcut, but as a force multiplier for debugging reactive code, reviewing JPQL logic, and shipping features faster without compromising quality.
