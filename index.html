A brief introduction.

Four years across fintech and event-tech, one obsession: making complex systems feel simple.

I started with a diploma in engineering and built my way up through curiosity — first into a B.Tech, then into production systems that serve thousands of users daily. Today I work across 30+ microservices at Kayana, owning features from API contract design through production rollout.
